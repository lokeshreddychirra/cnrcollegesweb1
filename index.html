About Us

Education that opens doors for rural talent

Located in Gudipala Mandal of Chittoor district, the CNR family of institutions is committed to making quality education accessible to students from villages and towns across the region.

C.N.R. Degree College offers undergraduate programs affiliated to Sri Venkateswara University, Tirupati. CNR School of Excellence lays the foundation with strong academics, values and holistic development.

  • Experienced, caring faculty
  • Discipline, values and character building
  • Affordable education close to home
  • Focus on academics, sports and skills
