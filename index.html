Step 1 · CNR School of Excellence

It starts with strong roots

Children begin at CNR School of Excellence, where they build the basics that everything else rests on: reading, mathematics, spoken English and good habits. When a child is struggling, a teacher notices and helps.

How we care

We know our students by name

Our students come from Gudipala and the villages around it. For many families, a college education is a new path. Our teachers stay close to every student, keep parents informed and make sure nobody is left behind on the climb.

  • Personal attention in class
  • Regular updates for parents
  • Guidance on courses and careers
  • Discipline with kindness

Step 2 · CNR Junior College

Choosing a direction

In Intermediate, students choose their stream and prepare for board exams with steady teaching, regular practice tests and teachers who explain until it makes sense.

Step 3 · CNR Degree College

A degree that opens doors

Since 2011, our degree college has offered programs affiliated to Sri Venkateswara University, Tirupati, with computer education built in. Graduates leave ready for work and for further study.

The summit

Reaching higher heights

From our campus, students go on to jobs, competitive exams and postgraduate study. Every student who climbs shows the next one what is possible. Your child's climb can start here.
