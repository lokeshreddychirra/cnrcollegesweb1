About CNR

A campus built for the students of our villages

The CNR Group of Institutions serves families across Gudipala Mandal and Chittoor district. We believe a child from a village deserves the same quality of teaching, discipline and opportunity as anyone in a big city.

C.N.R. Degree College offers undergraduate programs affiliated to Sri Venkateswara University, Tirupati. CNR School of Excellence builds strong foundations in the school years.
