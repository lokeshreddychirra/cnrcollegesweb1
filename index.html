Step 1 · CNR School of Excellence

It starts with strong roots

Children begin at CNR School of Excellence, where they build the basics that everything else rests on: reading, mathematics, spoken English and good habits. Festivals like Krishnashtami are celebrated on campus, so children grow up proud of who they are.

How we care

We know our students by name

Our students come from Gudipala and the villages around it. Teachers stay close to every student, keep parents informed and build discipline and confidence through activities like our cadet team.

  • Personal attention in class
  • Regular updates for parents
  • Cadets, sports and festivals
  • Discipline with kindness

Step 2 · CNR Junior College

Choosing a direction

In Intermediate, students choose the stream that fits their goal: MPC, BiPC, MEC or CEC in English or Telugu medium, or a job-oriented vocational course such as Accounting and Taxation, Computer Science or MPHW (Female) Nursing.

Step 3 · CNR Degree College

A degree that leads to a job

Our B.Voc in Animal Husbandry and Dairy Science prepares students for jobs in the Sachivalayam animal husbandry department and the dairy industry. Through our NSS unit, students also learn to serve: Not me, but you.

Step 4 · CNR College of Distance Education

It is never too late to learn

Stopped studying because of circumstances? Need a degree for a promotion? As an authorised study centre of Acharya Nagarjuna University and Sri Venkateswara University, we help people finish a degree or PG course while they work. Even without Intermediate, you can join a degree through an entrance test.

The summit

Reaching higher heights

From our campus, students go on to jobs, competitive exams and postgraduate study. Every student who climbs shows the next one what is possible. Your Ambition is our Motivation.
